Tips for consuming our streaming API

Chen · Aug 1, 2026

Last week we shipped the streaming API. Our team learned a few things the hard way while building the first clients, and this post collects those lessons so your integration starts smoother than ours did.

Why we went with SSE

Server-sent events keep the connection simple and play nicely with proxies. We compared websockets first, but SSE covered the use case with far less operational surface. The client only needs to parse text/event-stream and reconnect on drops.

const events = new EventSource("/v1/stream");
events.onmessage = (event) => append(event.data);

Our favorite debugging trick

Pipe the stream through a tee to watch bytes while your tests run. It sounds obvious but it saved us twice this month, especially for flaky proxy behavior in staging. The trick also works for the batching endpoint.

curl ... | tee /tmp/stream.log | jq -R 'fromjson?'

What we would do differently

We would ship a retry budget in the SDK from day one. Our first clients retried aggressively and amplified load during the beta. Next time, exponential backoff with jitter goes in the very first release, documented next to the timeout options in the API docs.
